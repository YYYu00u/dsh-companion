# v0.3.8 — let a thought finish

## Changed

- The inbound debounce starts at 4.2 s and adds up to 1.4 s of jitter, capped at 12 s. Sending two or three short messages in a row now produces one turn instead of three immediate replies.
- The first outbound line waits 0.45–1.35 s; later lines keep the per-character random gap. This keeps fast model replies from feeling mechanical.
- No always-on change: a single message still goes through after the quiet window.

## Verification

- `npm test`: 48 passing (delivery tests included).
- `npm run check`: clean.
- Manual: send three messages within ~5 s; one turn starts; wait 6 s with no follow-up; that message starts its own turn.
