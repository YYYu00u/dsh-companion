# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a place where people can build a small, personal thing without being harassed. That applies to contributors, maintainers, and users, regardless of experience, identity, or the kind of companion they want to build.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming a motive.
- Respect privacy. Do not ask people to share their persona, diary, chat logs, or API keys.
- Keep discussions about the project, not about whether someone's companion is "real" or "cringe".
- Accept that the maintainers may decline a feature that conflicts with the local-first, verified-only scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sexual attention directed at a person.
- Publishing someone's private data, including a home directory, persona, or chat log.
- Using issues/PRs to promote a competing product or to spam.
- Sharing explicit content in issues, screenshots, or test fixtures.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, PRs, discussions, and any project space. It does not govern what users do in their own homes.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via a private channel (see the repository profile). Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on severity and repetition.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific policy, informed by the Contributor Covenant, not a verbatim copy.
